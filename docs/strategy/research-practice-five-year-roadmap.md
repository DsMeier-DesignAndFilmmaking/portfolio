## Critical Decision Points

| # | Decision | Trigger | Recommended default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DP1** | **Does the wedge work?** *(the gate)* | Mo 3–12: 3 audits sold / 1 engagement closed? | Yes → proceed. No → re-scope the *offer or buyer* before anything else. Do not build past a broken wedge. |
| **DP2** | Niche depth vs. broaden | End of Yr 1 | **Depth.** Resist broadening until you own the niche. |
| **DP3** | Stay environmental vs. leap to horizontal AI governance | Yr 2–3 | **Stay.** Enter AI governance only through the stewardship origin, opportunistically — never as a cold pivot. |
| **DP4** | First hire — when & who | Yr 3, when *you* are the delivery bottleneck | Hire a **researcher/associate to extend you** — not a salesperson to replace founder-led selling (you must own sales early). |
| **DP5** | Build software / AI product | Yr 3–4 | Only when **all three** hold: method validated, demand pull, and it won't turn you into a SaaS company. License or partner before you build. |
| **DP6** | Scale studio vs. stay solo-elite vs. license IP *(the identity fork)* | Yr 4–5 | **Stay lean + senior, license the IP.** Optionality is the goal, not headcount. |

## Major Risks

1. **Over-planning, under-doing** *(the meta-risk this document embodies)* — the practice can plan indefinitely and never sell. **#1 risk.**
2. **Founder bottleneck / sales aversion** — if you won't sell, none of this happens.
3. **Proof never materializes** — frameworks stay hypotheses; the whole brand rests on evidence that doesn't arrive.
4. **Premature scaling** — hiring or building before proof burns the runway.
5. **Positioning drift** — sliding back into generic design-for-cash kills the niche.
6. **Cash runway in Year 1** — the unglamorous question: how do you eat while closing the first audits? Plan it explicitly.
7. **Single-beachhead concentration** — over-reliance on the outdoor-hospitality cycle.

- Other solo experts claiming the experiential×environmental intersection first — first-mover risk on positioning.

## Capabilities to Develop

Sales & founder-led BD *(the real gap)* · field-research rigor + a documented corpus · authority writing at cadence · a **teachable, transferable method** (so value isn't trapped in you) · outcome measurement (the evidence the brand rests on) · eventually, leading a small senior team.

---

## Capabilities to Deliberately Avoid

- **Becoming a software/SaaS company.** An evidence-and-authority practice is not an engineering org.
- **Generic design-services / agency drift.** The gravity well — easy cash, fatal to positioning.
- **Agency-scale full-time headcount.** Headcount is not the goal; leverage is.
- **A cold enterprise-AI-governance pivot.** No credible origin = no authority.
- **Bespoke-everything.** Custom work with zero productized leverage caps you at your own hours.
- **The influencer path.** Followers are not authority; proof is.
- **Chasing every adjacent vertical.** Focus is the moat.
- **Owning infrastructure/ops you could partner for.**

# The Founder's Strategic Playbook

## What This Practice Should Become

**The definitive independent authority on designing confidence into experiences — and the environmental systems that hold them.** Lean, evidence-driven, IP-led. Its frameworks become referenced standards; its work is the cited proof; its name owns one unclaimed intersection. World-leading by **depth, authority, and originality — not headcount.** The founder becomes a recognized voice holding the optionality to scale, license, or stay boutique-elite — a choice earned, not forced.

## How Success Should Be Measured

| Measure | Not |
|---|---|
| Validated case studies *(the master metric)* | Documents produced |
| Frameworks at RML 4+ | Frameworks invented-but-unused |
| % revenue recurring + % **not tied to founder hours** | Vanity revenue |
| Repeat + referral rate | Follower count |
| Inbound, citations, invitations *(authority, lagging)* | Activity / output volume |

## What Should Remain Constant (the 5-year strategic constants)

The niche · the wedge-led go-to-market · one idea repeated · the lean/IP-driven model · **proof before scale.**

## What Should Evolve

Delivery model · team · products · channels · pricing · the domains you serve · the founder's role (from doing → leading → defining).

## What Should Never Change (the soul)

- **Evidence honesty** — claims trace to observation; boundaries published. The most credible habit.
- **Human authority over automation** — the line you design and never cross.
- **The confidence + recovery thesis** — designing for confident action and graceful failure.
- **Refusal to overclaim** — the discipline that makes the authority real.
- **Craft and depth** — the reason anyone cites you at all.

## The Advisor's Closing Word

The summit is real: a world-leading practice owning an intersection no one else has claimed, built on frameworks the industry comes to reference. But you reach it through the least glamorous door — **selling three Confidence Audits and turning one into proof.** Year 1 is not preparation for the vision; Year 1 *is* the vision, in its only form that matters right now. Close the strategy chapter. Open the sales chapter. The next document this practice needs is a signed engagement.
