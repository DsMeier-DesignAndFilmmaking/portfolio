# Research Practice — Three-Year Growth Roadmap

**Goal:** Integrate all previous phases into a practical roadmap for growing the practice over the next three years — covering brand, portfolio, services, research, frameworks, products, markets, hiring, milestones, revenue, risks, and decision points.
**Date:** 2026-06-25

---

## Executive Summary

The nine prior analyses converge on one story: **a practice with exceptional thinking, no external proof, and no operating system around it.** The three-year job is to convert "interesting projects" into a compounding, revenue-generating, authoritative practice — without betting the brand on the capital-heavy moonshots.

The arc:

- **Year 1 — FOUNDATION ("become a practice").** Fix positioning, name and publish the canon (Architecture of Confidence + Ground Truth), launch the newsletter, document the corpus, and land the first beachhead pilot. Solo. *Goal: credibility, proof, first revenue.*
- **Year 2 — TRACTION ("productize and scale the ladder").** Turn the winning engagement into a repeatable service, launch the course and retainers, run one field study to completion (first validated proof), take opportunistic AI-governance work. First contractor. *Goal: recurring revenue, break the time ceiling.*
- **Year 3 — LEVERAGE ("IP income + studio optionality").** Book, certification, licensing; evaluate SaaS only on validated demand; decide solo-vs-studio. *Goal: scalable income beyond founder time.*

**The five non-negotiable first moves** (every prior doc points here): ① fix the four-names positioning, ② publish the AOC + Ground Truth pillars and a Methodology page, ③ document the 41-country corpus + launch the newsletter, ④ extract and name the Bounded Authority framework, ⑤ land one beachhead pilot. Everything else is rhythm and optionality on top of these.

## 36-Month Vision (Year 3 — Leverage)

By the end of Year 3, the practice is a **research-led authority** that no longer trades only time for money:

- **Brand:** owns the category language — "Architecture of Confidence" and "Ground Truth" are terms others use and cite. Optional studio identity if the founder chose to scale.
- **IP income:** a **published book** (anchored on AOC) drives inbound; **certification** (Confidence / Bounded Authority) and **framework licensing** generate income independent of founder hours.
- **Proof:** at least one framework validated with **measured outcomes (RML 5)** and external corroboration — the practice can credibly call itself research-backed, not concept-driven.
- **Products:** a **SaaS** (Adaptive-Hospitality ops tool or a HADE component) launched *only if* Year 1–2 demand validated it — otherwise deliberately deferred.
- **Org:** the founder has made a clear-eyed choice — a **small studio** (1–2 FTEs) or a **high-leverage solo authority** with contractors and IP income. Both are legitimate; the roadmap preserves the option rather than forcing it.
- **Markets:** horizontal decision-UX and AI governance as named verticals *on top of* the owned outdoor-hospitality beachhead.

Revenue mix target: balanced across services, recurring, and IP/licensing.

## Success Metrics

**Year 1 (Foundation) — leading indicators of becoming a practice**
- One settled practice name; Methodology page + 2 pillar essays live
- Newsletter launched; **500–1,500 subscribers**; corpus protocol documented + first tranche coded
- **2 case studies / pilots** underway or complete; first paid workshop delivered
- First revenue booked (~$80–150k illustrative); ≥1 framework page extracted (Bounded Authority)
- Evidence: one project moved to **RML 3** cleanly (AOC/ESD OS)

**Year 2 (Traction) — proof + recurring revenue**
- **1 field study to RML 4**; external bibliography in place (circular-sourcing broken)
- Repeatable productized service + **≥2 retainer clients**; course launched
- **3+ case studies**; speaking engagements delivered; newsletter **2,500–5,000**
- Revenue ~$150–300k; **≥25% recurring/product**; rates raised post-proof

**Year 3 (Leverage) — IP income + authority**
- Book published or under contract; certification live; ≥1 licensing deal
- **1 framework at RML 5** (measured outcomes) + external corroboration
- Revenue ~$300–500k+; **IP/recurring ≥ 40%**; founder-time per revenue dollar falling
- Category signal: external citations/uses of "Confidence" / "Ground Truth"; studio-vs-solo decision made deliberately

## Key Risks & Decision Points (consolidated)

| Risk | Mitigation | Gating decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash runway** during the low-revenue foundation year | Keep consulting/client work flowing; pilot-price for proof, not free | M6: is beachhead demand real enough to continue vertical-first? |
| **No validated proof** (RML 2.8) while selling | Document corpus + land pilots early; pair every claim with evidence boundary | M12: which offer converts — double down there |
| **Founder-time bottleneck** (single point of everything) | Productize; work-emits-content model; first contractor in Y2 | M24: studio/hire vs. stay solo-productized |
| **Three-business dilution** (hospitality + horizontal + AI) | Beachhead discipline; AI-gov opportunistic, not core identity | M18: invest in AI-governance or keep opportunistic? |
| **Cold-start audience** (no list) | Bootstrap publishing sequence (2 pillars → newsletter → atoms) | — |
| **Capital-heavy temptation** (HADE, Wayfinding, RE platform, SaaS) | Park as options; advance only on external capital/validated demand | M30: SaaS go/no-go |
| **Speculative-concept credibility** (jargon, psych claims) | Evidence boundaries + external citations; lead with the corpus | Ongoing: rate increases gated on case studies |

---

## Bottom Line

Three acts — **Foundation → Traction → Leverage.** Year 1 makes it a real, coherent, published practice with first proof. Year 2 makes it a repeatable, recurring business with validated research. Year 3 makes it an authority with IP income and a deliberate choice about scale. The whole plan rests on five first moves; do those in order and everything downstream compounds. The discipline throughout is the same one every prior audit identified: **lead with the framework, the method, and the corpus you already own — and defer the expensive builds until proof and demand justify them.**
