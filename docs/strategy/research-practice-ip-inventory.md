# Research Practice — IP Inventory & Roadmap

**Scope:** Treats every project in the independent research practice as an intellectual-property asset.
**Source projects:** The 9 `independent-research-practice` entries in `data/projects.ts`, plus their page/content sources.
**Date:** 2026-06-24

> **One structural insight shapes everything below:** these projects are not nine separate IP assets. They are a **layered IP stack with a shared spine.**

---

## Orientation: The practice is a 4-layer IP stack

| Layer | What it is | Projects |
|---|---|---|
| **L0 — Method** | How knowledge gets produced (the operating system) | Environmental Systems Design OS |
| **L1 — Platform framework** | The transferable core reused everywhere | The Architecture of Confidence |
| **L2 — Vertical architectures** | Domain-specific applications of L1 | Wayfinding Matrix · Intention Engine · Responsive Ecologies · Adaptive Outdoor Hospitality Companion |
| **L3 — Builds / prototypes** | Closest-to-shippable instantiations | HADE · Digital Executor · Field Notes |
| **Cross-cutting** | The primary-research corpus that feeds all layers | 41-country Field Notes corpus |

The single most important fact for IP strategy: **the Architecture of Confidence (L1) — and its sub-models (Confidence Assessment, Context Interpretation Loop, Recovery Architecture, Authority Routing) — is reused by Responsive Ecologies, Adaptive Ranch, Wayfinding Matrix, Digital Executor, and HADE.** That reuse is the asset. Protect, package, and lead with the framework, not the projects.

**Reading of the ranking:**
- **Tier 1 is the "monetize the thinking" tier** — framework + method + corpus. Capital-light, defensible, immediately usable for publications, workshops, and advisory. This is where to concentrate first; it converts the practice from "interesting projects" to "an operating practice" fastest.
- **Tier 2 is the "vertical consulting" tier** — these turn the framework into billable engagements in the practice's chosen domains (outdoor hospitality, ecotourism, stewardship). Responsive Ecologies is also the flagship that *proves the differentiation*, so it earns priority despite being capital-heavier.
- **Tier 3 is the "product bets" tier** — HADE and Wayfinding are the most exciting but most capital/hardware/AI-intensive and furthest from revenue. Keep them as research-grade demos that *advertise* Tier 1/2; don't fund full builds yet. HADE's ZK social graph is the one component worth an early defensive IP filing.

## 3. Long-Term IP Roadmap

### 3a. Disposition — what each asset should become

**Remain research (validate before claiming more):**
- **Wayfinding Matrix** — needs sensor/hardware + field validation; keep as concept + research roadmap.
- **Responsive Ecologies — the autonomous multi-agent *control* claims** — keep as research; the *frameworks inside it* graduate (below).
- **HADE — the ZK social graph & biometric inference** — validate technically/ethically before productizing.
- **Intention Engine — psychological inference** — ethics-sensitive; keep behind the stated ethics framework until guest-language studies run.

**Evolve into products:**
- **HADE** → adaptive decision engine (primary product path; file IP on ZK proximity first).
- **Field Notes** → place-aware capture app + structured field journal.
- **ESD OS** → licensable "research/evidence OS" template (Notion or SaaS) for other studios.
- **Confidence Assessment Model** → a decision-support design toolkit/scorecard.

**Become consulting methods (billable engagements):**
- **Architecture of Confidence** → "Confidence Audit" of a client's product/decision flow.
- **Adaptive Outdoor Hospitality Companion** → outdoor-hospitality experience + operations design (nearest-term cash).
- **Intention Engine** → experience-blueprinting service (the 9-dimension Transformation Blueprint is the deliverable).
- **Responsive Ecologies** → stewardship decision-system + authority/confidence modeling for outfitters/eco-lodges.
- **ESD OS audit method** → "Systems Audit" engagement.

**Become workshops:**
- **Architecture of Confidence** → "Designing for Confidence" (most teachable; flagship workshop).
- **ESD OS principles + evidence lineage** → "Evidence-Based / Traceable Systems Design."
- **Intention Engine translation method** → "Intention-to-Environment Blueprinting."
- **Responsive Ecologies confidence/authority model** → facilitation workshop for stewardship & ops teams.

**Become publications:**
- **Architecture of Confidence** → flagship long-form framework essay (and the spine of a future book).
- **Field Notes corpus** → field-journal essay/photo series across 41 countries (content engine + credibility).
- **ESD OS method** → "How to run an evidence-traceable design practice."
- **Responsive Ecologies** → case study on integrative, authority-aware AI systems design.
- **Intention Engine ethics framework** → POV piece on humane experience inference.

### 3b. Sequenced horizons

**Horizon 1 — Now → 6 months (Tier 1: monetize the thinking, capital-light)**
- Publish the **Architecture of Confidence** as a standalone framework piece; spin out the **"Confidence Audit"** advisory offer and the **"Designing for Confidence"** workshop.
- Package the **ESD OS method** as a public methodology page + a "Systems Audit" service.
- Stand up the **Field Notes** publication stream (essays/photo series) from the existing 41-country corpus.
- **One defensive action:** document authorship/dating of the core frameworks; file or formally record the **HADE ZK social-graph** concept.

**Horizon 2 — 6 → 18 months (Tier 2: vertical consulting + flagship)**
- Launch **outdoor-hospitality** and **experience-blueprinting** consulting built on Adaptive Ranch + Intention Engine; productize the Transformation Blueprint as a paid deliverable.
- Use **Responsive Ecologies** as the flagship case study in all biz-dev; run one real-property validation of its confidence/authority model.
- Pilot the **ESD OS** as a licensable template with 1–2 friendly studios.

**Horizon 3 — 18 → 36 months (Tier 3: selective product bets)**
- Fund a focused build of **HADE** (or the Field Notes product) only after Tier 1/2 revenue validates demand and the ZK component is protected.
- Treat **Wayfinding Matrix** and **Responsive Ecologies platform** as research-backed concepts that attract partners/grants rather than self-funded builds.
- Consolidate the framework publications into a book-length or signature long-form work — the practice's definitive IP statement.

---

## Bottom Line

The practice is sitting on one genuinely valuable, reusable, capital-light asset — the **Architecture of Confidence + the ESD OS method + the 41-country corpus** — wrapped in eight projects that are mostly *applications and advertisements* of it. The IP strategy is not "build the products." It's: **package the framework, the method, and the corpus into publications, workshops, and advisory first** (Tier 1), **fund the vertical consulting that the framework enables** (Tier 2), and **defer the capital-intensive builds until they're de-risked and protected** (Tier 3).
