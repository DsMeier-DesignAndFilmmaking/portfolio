# Research Practice — Framework Commercialization Evaluation

**Goal:** Treat every framework as a potential consulting methodology and evaluate it on originality, transferability, business usefulness, scalability, educational value, and cross-industry relevance — and its potential for workshops, training, certification, books, consulting, toolkits, and products.
**Method:** Framework-grade IP is distributed across projects (per the architecture review), not confined to the one project typed "Framework." This evaluation extracts and scores the seven distinct, transferable methodologies that actually exist.
**Date:** 2026-06-24

---

## The strategic insight (read this first)

Two things the prior audits under-weighted:

1. **The practice's single most commercially valuable framework is buried inside a land-stewardship project.** Responsive Ecologies contains a **confidence-scored, authority-routed, human-in-the-loop decision-governance model** (5 weighted dimensions → confidence bands → suppression rules → named authority + escalation). Stripped of the wilderness framing, that is *exactly* the hottest problem in enterprise right now: **how much authority do you give an AI agent, and how do you gate it?** It's the highest-day-rate, most-timely asset in the entire practice — and today it's dressed as trail maintenance.

2. **There's a fork between positioning and money.** The founder's *positioning* is outdoor/environmental hospitality. But the *most valuable frameworks* (Confidence, AI Decision-Governance, Recovery) are **horizontal and tech-facing** — bigger markets, higher rates, more competition. The vertical frameworks (Hospitality Blueprint, Wayfinding) match the brand but address smaller markets. This is a real choice, addressed in the roadmap.

## Deliverable 2 — Commercial Potential

**Tier 1 — Platform methodologies (build the business on these):**

- **A. Architecture of Confidence** — the flagship. "Design products people trust and act on" is a universal conversion/activation/trust problem. Sells as a **"Confidence Audit"** (productized engagement), a flagship **workshop**, a **card-deck toolkit**, and the spine of **the book**. Highest breadth; the practice's signature.
- **C. Bounded Authority** — the **highest near-term revenue and day-rate**. Enterprise AI-governance spend is board-level right now. Sells as **advisory** (how to deploy agents safely), enterprise **training**, a **scoring toolkit**, and credibly a **certification** ("Responsible AI decision design"). Risk: a fast-moving, crowding field — move quickly, and lead with the *confidence-gating + authority-routing* angle that's genuinely differentiated.
- **B. Recovery Architecture** — the **most under-exploited universal play**. Almost no one designs the failure/recovery path deliberately; "recovery as core infrastructure, designed before disruption" is ownable. Sells as a **resilience workshop**, **recovery-playbook toolkit**, and **consulting** tied to churn/trust/support-cost. Strong **book** on its own or as AOC's standout chapter.

**Tier 2 — Niche methodologies (real revenue in focused markets):**

- **E. Transformation Blueprint** — the deliverable (the 9-dimension blueprint) is **directly billable today** in experience-led sectors (hospitality, wellness, ecotourism, cultural, experiential retail). Best as **blueprinting consulting** + a **canvas toolkit** + **workshops**. Softer business metrics cap the horizontal appeal.
- **D. Evidence OS** — sells to *designers and research teams*, a smaller, meta market. Best monetized as a **toolkit/template product** (the Notion OS) and **design-research training** — and as the credibility substrate that makes the other frameworks sellable.

**Tier 3 — Vertical / capital-constrained:**

- **G. Adaptive Hospitality Blueprint** — narrow, but the **nearest cash for the founder's actual audience** (ranches, lodges, outfitters). Monetize as **vertical consulting + operator/staff training**. Not a book or product play.
- **F. Signal Matrix** — strong thinking, but applications are **hardware/context-heavy**, dragging scalability. Sells as **wayfinding/legibility consulting** + the matrix as a **toolkit**; not a horizontal product.

**Revenue-readiness (how fast to first dollar):** C and G are fastest to *consulting* revenue (C = highest rate, G = warmest audience). A and B are fastest to *workshop/toolkit* revenue. Books and certification are 18–36-month plays for any of them.

## Deliverable 3 — Framework Roadmap

**Phase 1 (0–6 mo) — Productize the thinking (capital-light, sell now).**
- Package **A (Architecture of Confidence)** as a flagship **workshop** + **"Confidence Audit"** consulting offer + a card-deck **toolkit**.
- Spin **C (Bounded Authority)** out of Responsive Ecologies into its own named **AI-governance advisory** offer + scoring **toolkit** — this is the land-grab; do it first while the AI moment is hot.
- Stand up **G (Adaptive Hospitality)** consulting/training for warm outdoor-hospitality leads (cash while the horizontal plays mature).
- Publish **A** and **C** as flagship essays to seed demand.

**Phase 2 (6–18 mo) — Build the catalog + first scaled products.**
- Launch **B (Recovery Architecture)** workshop + playbook toolkit.
- Convert **E (Transformation Blueprint)** into a paid **blueprinting engagement** + canvas; run it in 2–3 experience-sector projects.
- Ship **D (Evidence OS)** as a **licensable template product**.
- Begin **the book** (anchored on A, with B and C as major movements) — a book is the credibility flywheel for the workshops/consulting.

**Phase 3 (18–36 mo) — Authority assets (require market + proof).**
- **Certification** for **C** (and possibly **A**) — only after a cohort of completed engagements provides proof and case studies.
- Productized **assessment/governance tool** (SaaS) for **A/C** if consulting demand validates it.
- Consolidate the framework essays into the published **book**; use it to anchor a training curriculum.

> Sequencing logic (carried from the evidence audit): consulting and workshops sell on the *strength of the thinking* and can launch now at RML 2–3. **Books and certification require validation and case studies** — they come after Phase 2 fieldwork, not before.

## Deliverable 4 — Priority Improvements

What each top framework needs before it's fully sellable:

1. **Name and separate the AI-governance framework (C).** It has no consulting name and is trapped inside a wilderness project. Name it (e.g., **"Bounded Authority"** / "Confidence-Gated Autonomy"), give it its own page with a tech/enterprise framing, and strip the trail-maintenance context from the sales version. **This is the single highest-value packaging move in the practice.**
2. **Give every framework a clean one-line "job."** AOC = "design decisions people trust and act on." Recovery = "design the failure path before it fails." Each needs a single sentence a buyer repeats. Most are currently described by *what they contain*, not *what they do for you*.
3. **Build the missing apparatus each consulting methodology needs:** a **named deliverable** (Confidence Audit report, Recovery Playbook, Authority Map, Blueprint canvas), a **maturity/scoring instrument** (A and C already have scoring bones — finish them), and **a worked client-style example** for each. Buyers buy the artifact, not the theory.
4. **Add proof hooks.** Per the evidence audit, these are RML 2–3. For consulting that's acceptable *if* each framework shows one concrete application and an honest evidence boundary. Pair every framework with a worked case (even a self-run one) before pricing it.
5. **Resolve originality exposure on the soft ones.** E (Transformation Blueprint) leans on psychological states with no literature; F (Signal Matrix) overlaps known wayfinding/legibility theory. Cite the canon and state how you *extend* it — this protects originality claims and raises perceived rigor.
6. **Decide the horizontal-vs-vertical lead.** Recommendation: **lead horizontal (A, B, C) for revenue and rates; keep G/F as the proof that the horizontal frameworks work in the founder's signature environments.** Don't let the outdoor brand cap the addressable market for the platform IP.

---

## Bottom Line

The practice owns **three genuinely strong, horizontal, capital-light consulting methodologies** (Architecture of Confidence, Recovery Architecture, and a buried AI-governance framework worth surfacing immediately), plus a set of solid niche/vertical methods. The commercial path is **workshops + productized consulting + toolkits now** (Phase 1), **catalog + book + template products next** (Phase 2), and **certification + SaaS last** (Phase 3). The fastest, highest-value single action: **extract, name, and reposition the Bounded Authority framework** out of the stewardship project and into the AI-governance market.
