## Execution principles (the spine)

1. **Validation before construction.** No public brand/content asset gets built until a buyer has confirmed the pain and, ideally, paid for a fix.
2. **The market picks the beachhead, not the narrative.** Discovery decides the segment; we do not pre-commit to outdoor hospitality because it's a good story.
3. **Content is exhaust, not infrastructure.** Essays and the newsletter are byproducts of paid work, produced after there's something proven to write about.
4. **One hard rule:** *no new strategy documents until the first invoice is banked.* This plan is the last planning artifact.

## IMMEDIATE — 0–30 days · *"Make contact. Get one real signal."*

*Rationale: the practice has never met a buyer. Until it does, every downstream decision is a guess. Nothing here is a polished public artifact.*

| # | Initiative & objective | Deliverable | Depends on | Effort | Impact | Success metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Set the runway number + commitment decision** — how many months of survival; full-time or employed-with-side-practice | One number + one decision | — | 1 day | **High** | Decision made in writing |
| 2 | **Define ONE productized diagnostic** sellable on a call (fixed scope, fixed price, 2–3 wk) — in plain buyer language, *not* "Architecture of Confidence" | 1-page sell sheet | 1 | 1 day | **High** | Sell sheet a stranger understands in 30s |
| 3 | **Build a 40-name buyer list + outreach** across all candidate segments | List + sent outreach | 2 | 2–3 days | High | 40 contacted; ≥20 replies |
| 4 | **Run 20 buyer discovery interviews** — find where pain + budget + fast cycle coincide | Notes + 1-page synthesis | 3 | 3 wks | **Highest** | 20 completed; segments ranked by funded pain |
| 5 | **Minimum credible presence** — one-pager PDF + sharpened LinkedIn "about" (NOT a site rebuild) | One-pager + profile | 2 | 2–3 days | Med | Looks like a real practice, not a draft |

**Quick wins:** #2, #5. **Foundational:** #1, #4. **High-leverage:** #4.

> **DECISION GATE 1 (Day 30):** *Did discovery surface ≥3–5 buyers with a real, funded pain you can credibly address?*
> **Yes →** proceed to sell. **No →** the practice thesis is mis-aimed; re-segment, or materially re-weight the senior-role path. Do not proceed to build anything.

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Runway exhausted before first revenue** | High (if full-time) | High | Keep an income floor (part-time/contract); gate all spend to G2 |
| **Discovery surfaces no funded pain** | Med | High | Cast across 3 segments; decide fast at G1; re-weight the senior-role path |
| **Founder-aversion to sales/BD** | Med–High | High | Frame discovery as *research*, not selling; script the call; consider a BD-capable collaborator |
| **Beachhead has no budget** | Med–High | High | Let discovery (not narrative) choose the segment; be willing to lead with product/tech |
| **First delivery under-delivers** (solo ≠ "whole system") | Med | High | Scope the diagnostic narrow and solo-deliverable; don't promise whole-system in v1 |
| **Cold-start distribution fails** | High | Med | Borrow audiences; never depend on owned channels for first clients |
| **Reverting to planning (writing doc #14)** | High | High | Hard rule: no new strategy docs until first invoice — this plan is the control |

## Recommended Execution Sequence (linear)

```
Runway decision
    → diagnostic offer defined
    → 40-name buyer list built
    → 20 discovery interviews
    → [G1: funded pain confirmed?]
    → revenue model built
    → 3–5 proposals out
    → first engagement closed
    → first engagement delivered
    → [G2: offer works?]
    → offer productized
    → validated canon published
    → audience seeded (borrowed reach)
    → beachhead pilot + proof study
    → rates raised
    → [G3/G4: repeatable demand?]
    → path decision (studio / solo / senior role)
    → gated leverage (IP, book, cert — each gated)
```

Do not skip ahead. Each step de-risks the next. Building the canon before closing a sale is the failure mode the red-team identified.

## Bottom line

The strategy is approved. Execution means leaving the documents and entering the market within 30 days. The plan is built so its first four weeks generate real-world evidence, its first quarter generates an invoice, and nothing expensive gets built until the market has paid for it.

**The next action is not this document. It is the runway number and the sell sheet.**
