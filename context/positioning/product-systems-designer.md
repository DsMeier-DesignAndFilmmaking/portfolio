# Product Systems Designer — Core Positioning

## Core Identity

Daniel Meier is a Product Systems Designer who architects AI-aware decision systems for high-context, real-world environments — with a primary focus on travel, mobility, and ambient intelligence.

Not a UI designer who learned systems thinking. A systems thinker who designs interfaces as the output layer of deeper logic.

---

## Positioning Statement

> "I design the logic that makes experiences feel inevitable — not just the screens, but the inputs, rules, and feedback loops that drive them."

Specialized in the intersection of:
- Context-aware AI systems
- Travel and real-world mobility products
- Trust, transparency, and decision-making UX

---

## Key Differentiators

### Systems Thinking Over Screen Design
- Work starts from problem structure: inputs, constraints, logic, outputs
- UI is a surface for a decision system — not the system itself
- Can diagram, spec, and prototype full product logic before touching Figma

### Real-World Context Fluency
- Designs for messy, variable conditions: weather, crowds, energy state, time pressure
- Understands that real product behavior diverges from lab assumptions
- HADE is proof: built around real environmental telemetry, not hypothetical edge cases

### AI-Aware UX
- Treats AI as a runtime collaborator, not a bolt-on feature
- Designs trust layers, verification signals, and failure states for AI outputs
- Familiar with LLM orchestration patterns, ZK-proofs, retrieval-augmented decision systems

### Constraint-Driven Design
- Design decisions are motivated by real constraints: latency, privacy, cognitive load, battery
- Does not design for ideal conditions; designs for graceful degradation

## Background Reframing

### From: Freelance Designer
The surface read is a portfolio of client work across Intel, McDonald's, healthcare, and travel.

### To: System-Level Ownership
The accurate read is a designer who owned entire product logic flows — from problem definition through to shipped system behavior — without the safety net of a full product org. Freelance is not a lesser credential; it is evidence of end-to-end ownership under resource constraints.

**Reframe points:**
- Freelance = full product accountability with no hand-offs
- Client diversity = cross-domain systems fluency
- Spec-to-ship without a PM = product thinking embedded by default

## Narrative Anchor: HADE System

HADE (Holistic Adaptive Decision Engine) is the proof-of-concept that anchors the entire positioning.

It demonstrates:
1. The ability to design non-trivial system architecture (not just UI)
2. AI-first product thinking with trust and verification built in
3. Travel-specific domain depth
4. A real-time adaptive loop: observe → retrieve → synthesize → output → adapt

Use HADE as the entry point for every application and conversation about product systems design.
